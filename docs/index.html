    About the data & model

    Routes are computed on a directed street graph that respects one-way streets, OSM turn restrictions and a no-U-turn rule at intersections.

    Travel time = driving at 85% of the speed limit + a delay at every junction (3–10 s without traffic lights, 15–25 s with traffic lights, depending on straight / right / left). This gives about 21 km/h on average – an estimate for typical daytime conditions, not real-time traffic.

    Network load: 1,000 synthetic trips between random points in Vračar (fixed random seed) are routed with the same model. “Flow” is the number of simulated trips using a street – not a vehicle count. After a closure only the affected trips are re-routed; trips with no remaining route are reported separately.

    Street data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Simulated results only – not real traffic data.
